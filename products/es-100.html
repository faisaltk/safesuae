FIRE RESISTANT SAFE

Eagle Fire Resistant Safes ES-100

Ideal for home and business use, this fire-resistant safe has enough capacity to hold and protect your valuables and important documents from fire.

  • High-security, fire-resistant safe with proven certifications.
  • 1-hour fire protection certified by KS 1 hour (Furnace Temperature: 945°C) and SP NT-Fire 017 120P (Furnace Temperature: 1049°C).
  • Three-way main bolt work with independent relocking device.
  • Two to three stationary locking bolts on the hinge side to deter prying attacks on hinges.
  • Lockable drawer with adjustable shelf.
  • Wheels for easy moving.
  • Model: ES-100
  • Lock: 1 Digital + 1 Key Lock
  • External Dimensions: 1066(H) × 585(W) × 507(D) mm
  • Internal Dimensions: 833(H) × 426(W) × 346(D) mm
  • Weight: 200 kg
